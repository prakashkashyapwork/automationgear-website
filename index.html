How projects work

Understand first. Automate second.

A simple delivery approach keeps the solution tied to the way the business actually works.

01
Understand the taskReview the existing spreadsheet or repetitive workflow and identify the pain point.
02
Agree on rulesConfirm required fields, exceptions, expected outputs and what needs human review.
03
Build and testUse a practical mix of Excel work and Python automation, then test against sample data.
04
Deliver with clarityProvide the output and explain what was automated, what was flagged and what remains manual.

Designed for useful, measurable improvements.

Automation should make work more dependable—not hide errors behind a script. Validation rules and exceptions should be visible, and important decisions should remain with the people responsible for them.
